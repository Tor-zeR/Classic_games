Robo Maze — Game Guide & Strategy

Rating: ★★★★★ 4.6/5 (112 votes)

Robo Maze is an action-packed 8-directional shooter set in a series of deadly, electrified maze rooms, inspired by the 1980 arcade hit Berzerk. Navigate the maze, blast hostile security robots, and locate the exit doorways. Be prepared for Room 10, where you must locate and destroy the Master Switch to stop the mastermind boss "Brotto" once and for all.

How to Play

  • Move: Navigate in 8 directions using the Arrow Keys or WASD (or the virtual joystick on mobile).
  • Fire: Press the Spacebar or Z key to fire lasers in your current movement direction. Note: Firing locks your movement in place temporarily.
  • Escape: Eliminate the robots or run past them to find exit doors on the borders. Leaving a room takes you to the next maze layout.

Pro Strategies

  • Electrified Walls: Do not touch the electrified boundaries of the maze rooms. They cause instant death upon contact.
  • Robot Crossfire: Robots are not very smart and will shoot each other if they are aligned. Position yourself to trigger crossfire, conserving your ammo and keeping you safe.
  • Defeating Brotto: In Room 10, target the security terminals to deactivate boss Brotto's shields and hit the Master Switch to clean the grid.
