Tetrix — Game Guide & Strategy

Rating: ★★★★★ 4.9/5 (384 votes)

Tetrix is a modern neon reimagining of the legendary Tetris falling block puzzle game. Rotate, move, and drop geometric tetrominoes to form complete horizontal lines. Clearing lines increases your score and level, which speeds up the descent of the blocks.

How to Play

  • Move: Use the Left/Right Arrow keys (or swipe left/right on mobile).
  • Rotate: Use the Up Arrow key or Z key to rotate blocks (or tap/swipe up on mobile).
  • Soft Drop: Press the Down Arrow key to speed up descent (or swipe down on mobile).
  • Hard Drop: Press the Spacebar to drop the block instantly.
  • Pause: Press the P key to pause the game.

Pro Strategies

  • Build Flat: Keep your stack as flat as possible to avoid leaving deep vertical wells that are difficult to fill without specific blocks.
  • The Quad-Line Clear (Tetrix): Stack your blocks leaving a single empty column on the far right or left, then slide in a straight 4-block "I-bar" to clear 4 lines at once for a massive score multiplier.
  • Quick Decisions: As the speed increases with each level, plan where your next block (shown in the "Next" panel) will go before the current block lands.
