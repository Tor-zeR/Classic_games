## Tetrix
**Folder:** `tetris/` · **Theme:** Cyan · **Music:** CHIP (track 1) · **Orientation:** Portrait

Classic block-stacking game. Rotate and place tetrominoes to clear lines. Speed increases with level.

- 7 standard tetrominoes with wall-kick rotation
- Increasing speed per level
- Ghost piece preview
- Hold queue

## Neon Serpent
**Folder:** `snake/` · **Theme:** Orange · **Music:** CHIP (track 1) · **Orientation:** Landscape

Grow your snake by eating food. Don't hit walls or yourself.

- Dynamic wall obstacles appear as snake grows (regenerate only when full food batch is eaten)
- BFS reachability check — food is always accessible after walls spawn
- Shoot bonus, shield bonus, speed-slow collectibles
- Active bonus shown as DOM bar below the game field (name + countdown)
- Multi-food batches at higher lengths

## Robo Maze
**Folder:** `berzerk/` · **Theme:** Purple · **Music:** 8BIT (track 3) · **Orientation:** Landscape

Escape the robots in a maze of rooms. Shoot them before they shoot you — or Brotto gets you.

- Procedurally generated rooms with protected player spawn zones
- Brotto (the indestructible pursuer) activates after a delay, gets faster over time
- 8-directional movement and shooting
- Room transitions in all 4 directions
- **Master Switch** appears in room 10 only after all robots are destroyed
- **SFX:** room-enter sweep · robot shoot blip · Brotto appearance wail · Brotto movement pulse

## Gold Rush
**Folder:** `lode-runner/` · **Theme:** Red · **Music:** DUNGEON (track 4) · **Orientation:** Landscape

Collect all gold in the level and escape via the ladder. Dig through floors to trap guards.

- 10 handcrafted levels
- Dig left / dig right mechanic
- Guards fall into dug holes and respawn
- Ladder appears only when all gold is collected

## Territory

## Airborne
**Folder:** `paratrooper/` · **Theme:** Cyan/Green · **Music:** PATRIOT · **Orientation:** Landscape

Defend your base from paratroopers dropped by helicopters. Shoot everything before they land and assemble.

- Helicopters approach from both sides
- Paratroopers parachute down and walk toward the gun
- Auto-fire + manual fire modes
- Base is lost if enough paratroopers assemble
