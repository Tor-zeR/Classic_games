# About Page (`/about/`)

The site includes a dedicated **About & Mission** page accessible at [`/about/`](https://classicarcade.win/about/).

---

## Content & Overview

1. **Mission & Vision**: Detailed explanation of Classic Arcade's purpose — providing authentic, high-performance, 60fps retro arcade games directly in the browser with zero downloads, logins, or paywalls.
2. **Technology Stack**: Details on the lightweight, pure vanilla HTML5 Canvas + Web Audio API architecture.
3. **Synthwave Audio Engine**: Overview of the procedural music scheduler and custom drum/synth synthesis engine.
4. **PWA & Offline Capabilities**: Explanation of how the service worker (`sw.js`) enables full offline play.
5. **Fan-Made Disclaimer & Licensing**: Explicit statement of non-affiliation with original 1980s game publishers.

---

## Technical Details

- **Path:** `/about/index.html`
- **Dependencies:** Loads `../css/style.css` for CRT scanlines and neon styling, and `../js/common.js` for audio and high-scores topbar integration.
- **Navigation:** Accessible from the footer of `index.html` and all game pages.
