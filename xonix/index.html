Territory — Game Guide & Strategy

Rating: ★★★★★ 4.6/5 (87 votes)

Territory is a retro capture-the-board puzzle game inspired by classic arcade hits like Xonix and Qix. Move your probe off the safe outer border to draw trail lines and capture sections of the grid. Your objective is to fill and capture at least 75% of the total screen area. However, if any of the bouncing ball enemies touch your active trail before you reconnect with a border, you will instantly lose a life.

How to Play

  • Move Probe: Use the Arrow Keys (or swipe gestures on mobile) to steer the probe.
  • Draw Trails: Move off the blue filled areas into the dark grid to draw a trail line. Reconnecting with any filled territory captures the enclosed area.
  • Survive: Avoid letting the fast-moving balls hit your probe or your trail while it is active in the grid.

Pro Strategies

  • Small Bites: Do not try to cut off large sections of the grid at once. Small, quick captures near the borders are much safer and less likely to be intercepted.
  • Watch Ball Vectors: Bouncing balls move in predictable diagonal patterns. Time your moves when the balls are moving away from your planned path.
  • Power-Ups: Grab spawning power-up items inside the arena to trigger speed boost or freeze enemies, allowing you to capture large portions of territory safely.
