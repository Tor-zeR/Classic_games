Alien Wave — Game Guide & Strategy

Rating: ★★★★★ 4.7/5 (156 votes)

Alien Wave brings retro space combat to the modern web browser, drawing inspiration from Space Invaders and Galaga. Command a ground defense turret and defend Earth from rows of descending alien invaders. Dodge returning enemy laser fire and destroy the motherships (UFOs) that occasionally fly across the top of the screen for bonus points.

How to Play

  • Move: Use the Left/Right Arrow keys (or the touch controls on mobile) to position your starship.
  • Fire: Press the Spacebar (or tap the FIRE button on mobile) to launch laser bolts at the alien armada.
  • Dodge: Avoid incoming red laser drops from the invaders. Colliding with a laser costs you one defense turret life.

Pro Strategies

  • Bunker Cover: Hide underneath the green defensive bunkers to block incoming fire. Be mindful that bunkers disintegrate under repeated enemy or friendly hits.
  • Outer Columns: Target the outermost columns of the alien formation first. This widens their path and slows down their overall descent, giving you more time to clear them.
  • UFO Hunting: Always listen for the warning chime of an incoming mothership at the top of the screen. Hitting it yields massive mystery bonus score rewards.
