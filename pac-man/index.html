Chomp — Game Guide & Strategy

Rating: ★★★★★ 4.8/5 (219 votes)

Chomp is a neon-infused maze-chase game inspired by the legendary 1980 arcade classic Pac-Man. Guide Chomp through 4 distinct mazes, devouring every dot while dodging persistent ghost pursuers. Devouring large power pellets temporarily stuns the ghosts, turning them blue and vulnerable. Unique to Chomp, you can collect random ammo crates to unlock a laser weapon and shoot back at the ghosts!

How to Play

  • Move: Use the Arrow Keys (or swipe gestures on mobile) to guide Chomp through the maze corridors.
  • Eat Dots: Devour all dots in the maze to clear the level and advance to the next layout.
  • Power Pellets: Eat the flashing large circles to temporarily turn the ghosts blue. Run into blue ghosts to eat them for high scores.
  • Shoot Ghosts: Collect the bomb/ammo crates. Once loaded, press the Spacebar to shoot lasers at ghosts to temporarily vaporize them.

Pro Strategies

  • Ghost Train: Lure ghosts into a tight pack behind you before eating a Power Pellet. Eating them in rapid succession gives you exponentially higher points.
  • Use Tunnels: Utilize the side escape tunnels to warp to the opposite side of the screen. Ghosts travel much slower through these tunnels than Chomp, giving you space.
  • Save Ammo: Don't spam your laser as soon as you get ammo. Conserve your shots for when you are cornered or blocked.
