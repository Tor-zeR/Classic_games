Gold Rush — Game Guide & Strategy

Rating: ★★★★★ 4.7/5 (125 votes)

Gold Rush is a tactical action-puzzle platformer paying homage to the classic Lode Runner. Play as a neon treasure hunter, collecting all gold nuggets in the stage to reveal the escape ladder. You cannot jump—instead, use your digger guns to temporarily dissolve brick floors, allowing you to trap pursuing guards or create navigation paths.

How to Play

  • Move & Climb: Use the Arrow Keys or WASD (or the virtual D-pad on mobile) to walk, climb ladders, and hand-travel along overhead bars.
  • Dig Left: Press the Z key (or tap ◄ DIG on mobile) to blast a block to your left.
  • Dig Right: Press the X key (or tap DIG ► on mobile) to blast a block to your right.
  • Escape: Collect every gold pile in the level, then climb the green escape ladder that appears at the top of the arena to advance.

Pro Strategies

  • Trapping Guards: Dig a hole in front of a guard. When they fall in, they drop any gold they are carrying. You can walk safely over their heads while they are trapped.
  • Avoid Dead Ends: Digging holes requires time to regenerate. Do not dig yourself into a corner where you cannot climb out before the bricks solidify.
  • Level Restart: If you get completely stuck or trapped by guards, press the R key (or the level restart button in the pause menu) to retry the current layout.
